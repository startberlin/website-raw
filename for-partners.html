why partner with us?

connect with tomorrow's founders

START Berlin brings together the most ambitious, diverse, and entrepreneurial students from across 12+ universities in Berlin. As a partner, you gain direct access to this next generation of founders, investors, and innovators who are eager to engage with start-ups, venture capital firms and corporates.

hire the right talent

Looking for sharp minds and fresh perspectives? We help you reach highly motivated students ready to contribute as working students, interns, or full-time hires. Through our job board, exclusive events, and direct introductions, we make it easy and cost-effective to find the right fit.

join our european network

START Berlin is part of the wider START Global network with 20 chapters across Europe and South America. That means your partnership doesn’t just stay local—it connects you to a continent-wide community of entrepreneurial talent, including alumni who’ve built companies like SumUp and Trade Republic.

boost your brand visibility

Put your brand in front of the right audience—through branded events, social media, and curated content campaigns. With over 230,000 impressions and thousands of engagements each quarter, we make sure your message reaches students, founders, and VCs alike.

We’re part of the international START Network, which includes 15 chapters across Europe and South America. This connection allows us to exchange knowledge, collaborate internationally, and give our members access to the broader entrepreneurial ecosystem. Through the network, members can engage with a wide range of peers, attend international events like the START Summit, and gain insights from different regional startup environments.
