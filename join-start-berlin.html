Your time at START

A community
you contribute to.

Explore entrepreneurship with students who share your curiosity. Take responsibility in a department and help shape what START Berlin does.

  • Meet people beyond your university.

    Get to know fellow members and alumni. Find people to exchange ideas with or build something together.

  • Learn directly from the ecosystem.

    Join startup and VC visits. Take part in workshops with people who have built companies and invested in them.

  • Make projects happen.

    Organise events or develop our internal tools. Your department gives you a place to contribute and learn through practice.

  • Connect across the START Network.

    Meet peers from other chapters across Europe and Latin America, with opportunities to take part in international START programmes.

Who can apply

Bring your curiosity.
Commit your time.

  • You are currently enrolled at university.
  • You are passionate about entrepreneurship, whether you want to become a founder or explore venture capital and the startup ecosystem.
  • You will be based in Berlin for at least the next six months.
  • You are ready to contribute as an active member for one full year.

Before you apply

A few practical
details.

Do I need a startup idea to join?

No. You can apply if you are curious about entrepreneurship and want to contribute. You do not need an existing startup idea.

Do I have to record a video?

The application offers a short video or written answers. Both options are available, so choose the format that works for you. You will also be asked to upload your CV.

When will interviews take place?

Selected applicants will receive their interview details by email. Keep an eye on your inbox after applying.

How much time should I commit?

We ask you to contribute as an active member for one full year and be based in Berlin for at least the next six months. Your contribution depends on your department and projects, and we can discuss this in your interview.

Can I request support with my application?

If you need an adjustment to the application process, or have a question about your eligibility, email recruiting@start-berlin.com.
