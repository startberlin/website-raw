START-UP VISITS

What better way to learn about entrepreneurship than from successful and active founders?

We connect with inspiring entrepreneurs for conversations about their journeys. These engaging discussions reveal personal founder stories while providing valuable insights into startup histories, strategies, challenges, and victories.

Gain practical knowledge directly from those actively building businesses today and discover the authentic stories behind entrepreneurial success.

VC visits

The world of VCs can seem like a black box. Instead of guessing what investors are looking for, we ask them directly in our VC visits.

You'll engage in conversations with investment professionals who provide valuable insights into their decision-making processes, investment strategies, and what makes startups stand out.

These exclusive sessions demystify venture capital and reveal the human side of funding.

workshops

We hope you're hungry for knowledge.

Our workshops bring industry professionals directly to you, sharing their expertise through interactive, hands-on sessions. We select specialists from essential entrepreneurial domains — legal frameworks, marketing strategies, go-to-market execution, financial planning, and beyond.

Come ready to participate, ask questions, and develop valuable capabilities that will enhance your entrepreneurial journey.

COMMUNITY

START Berlin brings together an exclusive, handpicked community of ambitious students.

Our community events create the perfect environment for students interested in startups, venture capital, and business to build meaningful connections. Join our community to exchange ideas, build your professional network, and position yourself among Berlin's next generation of business leaders—all while surrounded by peers who match your entrepreneurial vision and drive.
